Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16-18 September 2026 • Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

About the Expo

Organised by the Ministry of Agriculture, Animal Industry and Fisheries (MAAIF), the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 brings together coffee farmers, roasters, exporters, agronomists, and international buyers in Kampala. The three-day event focuses on expanding value addition, improving post-harvest handling, and positioning Ugandan Arabica and Robusta coffees in global specialty markets.

Participants will experience live cupping sessions, machinery demonstrations, policy roundtables, and direct business-to-business matchmaking forums along the shores of Lake Victoria. Whether you are a smallholder cooperative leader or an international trade representative, the expo offers practical insights and partnerships to grow the coffee value chain.
