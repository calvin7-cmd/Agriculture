Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16-18 September 2026 • Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

Featured Keynote Speakers

Dr. Grace Nanteza

Director of Crop Research, National Coffee Research Institute (NaCORI)

Dr. Nanteza leads breeding programs focused on drought-tolerant and disease-resistant Robusta varieties across Uganda. She has published widely on sustainable agroforestry systems for smallholder farms.

David Mukasa

Managing Director, Pearl Origin Coffee Exporters

David has over 18 years of experience connecting Rwenzori and Elgon farmer cooperatives directly with specialty roasters in Europe and Asia. He advises regional trade bodies on quality grading standards.

Henrik Lindqvist

Head of Sustainable Sourcing, Nordic Roast Group

Henrik oversees ethical procurement and supply chain traceability across twelve coffee-producing nations. His work focuses on helping producer organizations meet European environmental compliance standards.

Sarah Namukasa

Lead Q-Grader & Founder, Kampala Coffee Lab

Sarah is a certified Q-Grader who trains young Ugandan cuppers and roasters in sensory evaluation and post-harvest fermentation techniques. She regularly serves as head judge at regional quality competitions.

Call for Papers

Researchers, agronomists, economists, and industry practitioners are invited to submit abstracts for technical presentations and poster sessions at the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026. Submissions should address practical innovations in coffee genetics, climate adaptation, post-harvest processing, or international market access.

Abstracts must be written in English, not exceed 300 words, and clearly outline the methodology, key findings, and relevance to the coffee sector. All accepted papers will be published in the official MAAIF Expo Proceedings and presented during the parallel technical tracks.
