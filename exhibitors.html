Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026

16-18 September 2026 • Speke Resort Munyonyo, Kampala

Exhibitor Booth Registration

Thank you for your interest in exhibiting at the Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026. Booth allocations for producer cooperatives, roasting equipment suppliers, exporters, and financial institutions are currently open through the MAAIF Secretariat.

Standard exhibition booths measure 3m x 3m and include shell scheme walls, power supply, two exhibitor passes, and listing in the official expo directory. Contact expo@agriculture.go.ug to reserve your space.
